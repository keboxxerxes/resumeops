## Rationale corrections verified

The prior scan was not fabricated, but a passing scan with 15 skips does not establish those controls exist. The builder corrected, and the reviewer independently verified, the following inaccurate or incomplete explanations in inline skips and operating documentation: logging as inherently expensive; smoke checks as access logging; lockfiles as event monitoring; same-region versioning as regional disaster recovery; an operator backup as already verified; and standalone WAF pricing as a claim that every CloudFront WAF offering costs extra. No mandatory public-block, encryption, versioning, OAC, trust, IAM, approval or workflow control may be suppressed to make a scan pass. Final Checkov rerun after those corrections: exit 0, 67 passed, 0 failed, 15 precisely scoped skips, 0 parsing errors. Exact commands and full 15-pair inventory are in `reviewer/continuation-commands.json` and `reviewer/continuation-exceptions.json`. No unjustified suppression remains within the original specification. Final closure and complete-suite evidence are recorded in SECURITY_REVIEW.md.

## Cost recheck under the unchanged specification

The target remains **below USD 1/month at low traffic**; the budget remains **USD 5/month**, with private **ACTUAL** notifications at USD 1/3/5. None is a hard cap. The following are public price checks, not account eligibility or live billing verification.

- [Official S3 us-east-1 Price List](https://pricing.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/offers/v1.0/aws/AmazonS3/current/us-east-1/index.json), published 2026-11-21, independently retrieved: first-tier Standard USD 0.023/GB-month; PUT/COPY/POST/LIST USD 0.005/1,000; GET USD 0.004/10,000. The exact public entries are saved in [continuation-pricing.json](reviewer/continuation-pricing.json).
- [CloudFront pay-as-you-go](https://aws.amazon.com/cloudfront/pricing/pay-as-you-go/) currently lists North American marginal USD 0.085/GB and USD 0.0100/10,000 HTTPS requests, plus monthly always-free allowances of 1 TB/10 million requests. This is distinct from flat-rate plans; account allocation/eligibility still needs operator verification. Existing illustrative usage gives about USD 0.1063 before allowances, state overhead and tax, consistent with the target rather than a guarantee.
- [AWS Budgets](https://aws.amazon.com/aws-cost-management/aws-budgets/pricing/) monitoring and notifications are free; this project does not configure paid report delivery or automated budget actions.
- [WAF pricing](https://aws.amazon.com/waf/pricing/) through 2027-01-31 lists USD 5/web ACL-month plus USD 1/rule-month and request charges. The announced 2027-02-01 standalone model uses USD 0.01/protected-resource-hour, roughly USD 7.30/month. Both exceed the target before site traffic. This does not negate the separate free CloudFront flat-rate option or authorize redesign/enrollment.
- [KMS pricing](https://aws.amazon.com/kms/pricing/) lists USD 1/month for a customer-managed key. AWS-managed key storage is free, with usage charges; a blanket assertion that all KMS use has the same monthly fee is incorrect.

These choices do not remove billable-traffic risk, audit blind spots or regional recovery limits. Before any authorized live release, recheck current product rates, free allowances, expected traffic and alert delivery. No AWS credentials, account API, infrastructure plan, resource creation or publication was used for this price/exception review.
